About our hotel

Hospitality for your wellbeing.

A 130-seat restaurant

The hotel restaurant seats 130 and offers a pleasant atmosphere and varied dishes, adapted to guests following treatment programmes or looking for a balanced diet.

Pool and treatment centre

The hotel has a heated indoor pool, ideal for relaxing in any season, and a modern treatment centre fully equipped with state-of-the-art BTL devices. Easy access to all the hotel's facilities makes for a complete and comfortable stay.

204 beds, rated 3 stars

Hotel Codru Moma*** has 204 beds, rated 3 stars, across 100 double rooms and 2 spacious apartments. The rooms are designed for comfort, practicality and quiet, and suit both short breaks and longer stays.
