About the hotel

Welcome!

Hotel Codru Moma*** welcomes you to a quiet natural setting in the Moneasa resort, a place dedicated to relaxation, recovery and wellbeing. Designed to balance modern comfort with the benefits of spa treatment, the hotel is the ideal choice for restful stays as well as treatment or medical recovery.

The welcoming atmosphere, quality services and attention given to every guest make each stay a pleasant experience, where caring for your health blends harmoniously with relaxation.
