Offers and packages

Autumn at Hotel Codru Moma

In autumn, the Moneasa resort, with its clean mountain air and copper landscapes, is the right place to regain your energy and wellbeing. Whether you want a holiday devoted to your health or a relaxing break in nature, we have prepared special packages.

RON 215–225 per person per day

Stay with treatment included

1 November – 20 December 2026

Look after your body before winter arrives. In our modernised treatment centre, with new BTL medical equipment and a team of professionals, we offer complete recovery packages.

  • Length of stay: at least 5 nights
  • Lodging (a place in a double room) with full board: RON 225 per person per day for 5–9 nights, RON 215 per person per day for 10–14 nights
  • Meals follow a set menu
  • Single occupancy supplement: RON 60 per day
  • Resort tax not included: RON 1 per person per day
  • Pool access is charged separately: RON 20 per person per day, or a pass
  • Last check-in day: 7 December 2026
  • Treatment: 5 to 10 days, 4 procedures per day per person
  • Provided with a referral from a GP or specialist and a health insurance card; the referral must state RECOVERY (RECUPERARE)
  • Treatment must take place within the same calendar month, Monday to Friday, 08:00–16:00, within the monthly amount allocated by the Health Insurance House (CAS)
