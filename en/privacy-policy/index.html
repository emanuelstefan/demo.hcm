Privacy policy (GDPR)

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. General information

Protecting your personal data is a priority for us. This Privacy policy describes how personal data of users of the Hotel Codru Moma website is collected, used and protected, in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 on data protection (GDPR).

By using this website and completing its forms, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data controller

Personal data controller: T.B.R.C.M. S.A. – Sucursala Moneasa (hereinafter “Hotel Codru Moma”).

For any data protection questions, please use the contact details on the website’s Contact page.

3. What personal data we collect

Through the website, in particular the pre-booking form, we may collect:

  • first and last name
  • e-mail address
  • phone number
  • requested room type
  • preferred period of stay
  • comments voluntarily provided by the user
  • technical data recorded automatically by the hosting server (e.g. IP address, browser type, pages accessed)

We do not collect sensitive data (e.g. medical data) through the website. Please do not include such data in the comments field.

4. Purpose of processing

Personal data is collected and processed only to:

  • manage pre-booking and contact requests
  • communicate with users to confirm availability
  • provide requested information about services and stays
  • improve the operation and security of the website
  • comply with the controller’s legal obligations

5. Legal basis

Personal data is processed on the following legal bases, under Article 6 GDPR:

  • the data subject’s consent (by completing the form)
  • steps taken at the data subject’s request before entering into a contract
  • the controller’s legitimate interest in answering requests and keeping the website working correctly
  • legal obligations, where applicable

6. How long data is kept

Personal data is kept:

  • for as long as needed to handle the pre-booking request
  • afterwards, for a reasonable period, for record-keeping and in line with legal obligations

7. Recipients

Personal data may be accessed or processed by:

  • authorised Hotel Codru Moma staff
  • IT service providers (hosting, website maintenance)
  • e-mail or technical infrastructure providers

Data is not sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

8. Transfers outside the EU

Data is not normally transferred outside the European Economic Area. If certain technical services involve such transfers, they are carried out under the safeguards provided by the GDPR.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • the right of access
  • the right to rectification
  • the right to erasure (“the right to be forgotten”)
  • the right to restriction of processing
  • the right to object
  • the right to data portability
  • the right to withdraw consent, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing

To exercise these rights, please use the contact details on the website.

10. Data security

Hotel Codru Moma applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure.

11. Cookies

The website does not use cookies. Details are in the Cookie policy.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy policy may be updated periodically. The updated version will be published on this page.
