Rooms and apartments

Comfort starts with the right room

The rooms at Hotel Codru Moma*** are arranged to be pleasant, practical and relaxing, ideal for both short breaks and longer stays.

Double room

1–2 guestsDouble bed

At 15.5 sqm, the double rooms suit one or two guests. They come with a SMART TV, telephone, mini-fridge and balcony, offering a comfortable space to rest and relax.

Room details

Twin room

2 guestsSeparate beds

Also 15.5 sqm, the twin rooms are ideal for two guests who prefer separate beds. Amenities include a SMART TV, telephone, mini-fridge and balcony, for a pleasant and quiet stay.

Room details

Apartment

1–4 guestsLiving room + bedroom

At 31 sqm, the apartments are divided into a living room and a bedroom, suiting 1–4 guests. They have a SMART TV, telephone, mini-fridge and a generous 25 sqm terrace, for extra space and comfort.
