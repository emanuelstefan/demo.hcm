Services

Services and treatments for your recovery

Our services are built around what guests really need, be it rest, treatment, exercise or time outdoors. We care about safety, a suitable pace and a calm atmosphere, so that everyone can enjoy a balanced and pleasant experience.

Lodging in rooms and apartments

Hotel Codru Moma*** has 204 beds, rated 3 stars, across 100 double rooms and 2 spacious apartments. The rooms are designed for comfort, practicality and quiet, and suit both short breaks and longer stays.

See the rooms

Food and balanced meals

Complete meals suited to your kind of stay, with local dishes and Romanian cuisine, including breakfast, lunch and dinner options. The hotel restaurant seats 130 and offers a pleasant atmosphere and varied dishes, adapted to guests following treatment programmes or looking for a balanced diet.

Indoor pool with mesothermal water

Access to an indoor pool with heated water (31–33°) for relaxation and hydrokinetic therapy (HKT) in a pleasant setting. A modern 50 sqm pool, 1.5–1.7 m deep. The pool area has separate changing rooms for women and men, and showers.

Treatments, therapeutic procedures and recovery

Our treatment centre offers modern therapies such as TECAR, magnetotherapy, high-intensity laser, electrotherapy and kinesiotherapy, alongside classic spa procedures and therapeutic massage. All therapies are carried out under specialist supervision, according to each patient's needs.

All procedures

Medical consultations for treatment

Every treatment plan is based on an initial consultation with our balneology doctor.

Recreational activities

Walks and easy hikes in the green surroundings of Moneasa, clean air, quiet mountain scenery, small artistic and folk moments, plus outdoor games to socialise and lift the mood.
