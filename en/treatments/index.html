Treatment centre

Therapeutic procedures adapted to individual needs

Our treatment centre offers a wide range of therapeutic procedures, using modern equipment and under specialist supervision. They aim to support functional recovery, reduce discomfort and improve mobility, according to each patient's needs.

The procedures we offer

  • TECAR therapy

    A modern therapy using radiofrequency currents to stimulate tissue regeneration, reduce pain and speed up recovery.

  • Magnetodiaflux (MDF)

    A procedure based on pulsed magnetic fields, recommended for reducing inflammation, relieving pain and stimulating circulation.

  • High-intensity laser – HILT therapy

    An advanced therapy working in depth, with anti-inflammatory, analgesic and biostimulating effects, ideal for muscle and joint conditions.

  • Electrotherapy

    Includes diadynamic, Trabert, TENS and interferential currents, used to ease pain, relax muscles and stimulate peripheral nerves.

  • Electrostimulation (Electrospastic)

    A procedure used for muscle toning and functional recovery, especially in neurological or post-traumatic conditions.

  • Shortwave and ultrasound

    A therapy with a deep thermal effect, helping to reduce muscle contractures and improve joint mobility.

  • Paraffin

    Applied locally, paraffin has a relaxing, anti-inflammatory effect and is especially recommended for rheumatic conditions of the hands and feet.

  • Four-cell galvanic baths

    A procedure used in circulatory and neurological conditions, with a stimulating and relaxing effect.

  • Hydromassage and individual mesothermal water baths

    Helps relax muscles, reduce stress and improve circulation.

  • Therapeutic massage

    Recommended for reducing muscle pain, relaxing tissue and improving mobility.

  • Relaxation / anti-cellulite massage

    Helps release tension, promotes general relaxation and improves the look of the skin.

  • Kinesiotherapy

    Personalised exercise programmes for recovery, maintaining mobility and correcting posture.

  • Hydrokinetic therapy

    Exercises in water, ideal for gentle and effective recovery with little impact on the joints.

  • Phototherapy – infrared (IR) therapy

    A therapy with a deep warming effect, recommended for relaxation and pain relief.

  • Lymphatic drainage – pressotherapy

    An effective procedure for stimulating lymphatic circulation, reducing oedema and detoxifying the body.

The treatment centre

The treatment centre at Hotel Codru Moma*** is a space dedicated to health, recovery and physical balance, on the hotel's ground floor for quick and easy access. Equipped with state-of-the-art BTL medical devices, it offers modern, safe and effective services, supervised by a specialised medical team.

Treatment programmes are personalised to medical recommendations and each patient's needs, aiming to reduce pain, improve mobility, restore function and raise quality of life.

The treatment centre is recommended for

  • inflammatory rheumatic conditions (rheumatoid polyarthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, reactive arthritis, collagen diseases)
  • degenerative rheumatic conditions (gonarthrosis, coxarthrosis, spondylodiscarthrosis)
  • peripheral and central neurological conditions (ischaemic or haemorrhagic stroke, Parkinson's disease)
  • post-traumatic conditions
  • conditions caused by working under stress
  • asthenic neuroses and marked fatigue

Procedures and therapies can be covered by the Health Insurance House, within available funds. More details on the page Useful information.
