All you need to know before your stay

Stay with a ticket issued by the County Pension Houses

To obtain a treatment ticket from the County Pension Houses (a 16-day stay), please note the following:

  1. Submit the application to your County Pension House, on paper or by e-mail (details on online submission are on the Pension House website), together with a copy of your ID card and of your pension slip.
  2. On the application, enter Moneasa – Hotel Codru Moma and the series you would like. The series schedule is published on our website, our Facebook page and at the Pension Houses. Each series has fixed arrival and departure days.
  3. To receive the ticket you must present a copy of the GP referral (it must be within its validity of 60 days from issue) and 50% of your pension, if a contribution applies. Keep a copy of your ID card and latest pension slip to hand.
  4. Present the ORIGINAL treatment ticket at reception on the date stated on it, with the ORIGINAL GP referral, your ID card and pension slip.
  5. The ticket includes 16 nights' lodging, 12 days of treatment with a consultation by our balneology doctor and 3 procedures a day (Monday to Friday, 8:00–16:00), and 16 days of full board (breakfast, lunch, dinner) with set menus.
  6. The 16-day stay starts on the date stated on the ticket. Arriving late or leaving early means losing those days.
  7. Lodging is in a double room; the ticket holder is given one place in a double room. If you come alone, you will share the room with another guest.
  8. You are entitled to one treatment ticket a year.
  9. Tickets are awarded on a points system set by the County Pension Houses.
  10. People who worked in the Ministry of Internal Affairs system are not eligible for tickets issued by the County Pension Houses.

Note

  • If your county has no tickets for Hotel Codru Moma, or they have run out, please contact us and we will look for a solution.
  • For FREE treatment tickets, see the conditions on the County Pension House websites.

Stay with treatment covered by the Health Insurance House

If you choose a stay with treatment covered by the Health Insurance House, based on a referral, please note the following:

  1. With a referral we provide an initial consultation, a follow-up consultation and treatment with 4 procedures per person per day for 10 days, Monday to Friday (8:00–16:00).
  2. Procedures require the ORIGINAL referral and your health insurance card.
  3. If you are employed, bring an ORIGINAL certificate from your employer.
  4. Rates for lodging and meals (3 meals a day) are in the offers section of the website.
  5. Ideally the stay should start on Sunday, or on Monday by 11:00 at the latest, so that treatment can also take place on Monday.
  6. Single occupancy is subject to availability and carries an extra cost.

Important

  • The whole treatment MUST take place within the same calendar month, and we recommend starting on a Monday.
  • As treatment is covered by the Health Insurance House, funds are limited, so we recommend booking early.
  • With a GP referral you can have 2 stays with treatment included, provided the referrals are issued at least 6 months apart. Otherwise we cannot claim the treatment.
  • This rule also applies if you had treatment covered by CAS at another treatment centre or clinic.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Hotel Codru Moma?

Hotel Codru Moma*** is at Strada Principală 118, in the Moneasa resort, Arad County, Romania.

What types of rooms does the hotel have?

The hotel has 204 beds in 100 double rooms (double or twin, 15.5 sqm, with balcony) and 2 apartments of 31 sqm, with living room, bedroom and 25 sqm terrace.

Can my treatment be covered by the Health Insurance House?

Yes. With an original referral and a health insurance card we provide a consultation and 4 procedures a day for 10 days, Monday to Friday, 8:00–16:00, within available funds. The whole treatment must take place in the same calendar month.

What does a Pension House ticket include?

The ticket includes 16 nights' lodging, 12 days of treatment with a consultation and 3 procedures a day, plus 16 days of full board with set menus. Lodging is one place in a double room.

Which conditions is the treatment centre recommended for?

Inflammatory and degenerative rheumatic conditions, peripheral and central neurological conditions, post-traumatic conditions, conditions caused by working under stress, asthenic neuroses and marked fatigue.

Does the hotel have a pool?

Yes, a heated 50 sqm indoor pool, 1.5–1.7 m deep, with separate changing rooms. Access is charged; reception can tell you the current rates and hours.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, card, and holiday vouchers and cards.

Are parking and internet free?

Yes, parking is free, and internet is free throughout the hotel.

Does the website form confirm my booking?

No. The form is a pre-booking request. A booking is only firm once the hotel confirms it. If you hear nothing within 48 hours, please call us.
